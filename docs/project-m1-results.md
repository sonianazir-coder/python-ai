# Module 1 Project: AI Test Results

**Business:** Sweet Crumbs, bakery, Lahore
**Model:** gemma3:1b

## Prompt 1: Marketing

**Prompt:**

Write a short Instagram post for Sweet Crumbs, a bakery in Lahore.
Target university students. Use a friendly tone and include 1 call to action.

**AI answer:**

The AI suggested Instagram post ideas for university students, including
study treats, pastries, cakes and cookies. It also suggested using local
hashtags and inviting students to place orders through direct messages.

**Useful?**

Yes, the ideas are useful for marketing a bakery to university students.
However, the AI gave several options instead of one post. It also changed
the business name spelling in some places and included a shop link that
was not provided. These details should be checked before publishing.

## Prompt 2: Customer Service

**Prompt:**

You are the manager of Sweet Crumbs. Write a polite reply to a customer
whose order arrived late. Apologise and offer a small discount.

**AI answer:**

The AI wrote a polite apology for a late order. It suggested offering a
10% discount on the next order and explained how the business could thank
the customer and improve their experience.

**Useful?**

Yes, the response is polite and suitable for customer service.
However, the discount percentage and coupon code were examples suggested
by the AI. The business should confirm its actual discount before using
the reply.

## Prompt 3: Growth

**Prompt:**

Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a bakery in Lahore can use to attract university
students with a budget of 15000 PKR per month.

**AI answer:**

The AI suggested Canva AI, Jasper.ai and Summarizer.bg for marketing
content, captions and summaries. It also recommended starting with free
options and checking the value of paid tools.

**Useful?**

The suggestions provide ideas for creating marketing content.
However, the tool prices and features may be inaccurate or outdated.
The bakery should verify current pricing and features before spending money.
The AI did not prove that all the suggested tools fit the budget.
