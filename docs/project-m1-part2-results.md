## Test 1: Cake Recommendation

### Prompt

You are the assistant of Sweet Crumbs Bakery.
Answer Customer in 2 short lines.
Question: Which cake is best?

### Ollama Answer

We have several popular options, but I'd recommend our Strawberry Sunshine Cake or our decadent Chocolate Lava Cake. Both are fan favorites, but it ultimately depends on your personal taste preference!

### Evaluation

The answer is helpful because it recommends two cake options and allows the customer to choose according to personal taste. However, the bakery should confirm that these cakes are actually available before recommending them.


## Test 2: Late Order

### Prompt

You are the assistant of Sweet Crumbs Bakery.
Answer Customer in 2 short lines.
Question: My order is late. What should I do?

### Ollama Answer

I apologize for the delay, can I offer you a complimentary pastry to make up for it? Please allow me to check on the status of your order and I'll have it ready for you as soon as possible.

### Evaluation

The answer is polite and offers to check the order status. However, it promises a complimentary pastry without confirmation, so the bakery should verify this offer before making such a promise.
